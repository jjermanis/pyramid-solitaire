My mom asked me if I'd make a version of Pyramid Solitaire. Not a problem:
my family asks for a game with moderate difficulty to make, and I'm there.
This one I'm using Google Gemini. It's a standard game, with some variations.
I asked Gemini to consider all the variations and ask me to provide the 
answers. It did, then I did. Then I wrote up an initial spec with all those
variations made clear. I also included the Clock Solitaire game (also
made via Gemini) as the basis for UI. The initial attempt was impressive!
For sure, it is playable. It even did something slick and build with the MIT
License link.
I do see some changes. I will document and fix issues from here.
