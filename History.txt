Version 01.01	25 minutes dev	1 minute AI	4 minutes test		30 minutes

My mom asked me if I'd make a version of Pyramid Solitaire. Not a problem:
my family asks for a game with moderate difficulty to make, and I'm there.
This one I'm using Google Gemini. It's a standard game, with some variations.
I asked Gemini to consider all the variations and ask me to provide the 
answers. It did, then I did. Then I wrote up an initial spec with all those
variations made clear. I also included the Clock Solitaire game (also
made via Gemini) as the basis for UI. The initial attempt was impressive!
For sure, it is playable. It even did something slick and build with the MIT
License link.
I do see some changes. I will document and fix issues from here.

Version 02.02	21 minutes dev	1 minute AI	4 minutes test		1 hour 8

It was pointed out that an Undo button would be handy. You could draw another
card, then realize that card had a move. You'd have to cycle through the whole
deck to get back to it. An Undo would make that quick and easy.
To implement this, similar other games, I wanted to put Undo in the lower-left
corner. But I didn't want the draw pile and the Undo to be so close. To avoid 
that, I reverse the three card stacks on the button. After this Discard would
be over Undo. Which is fine: there is no action ever to take on Discard. The
draw and stock piles would be flipped on the right-side. This aligns them more
like they were done in my Klondike solitaire.
Gemini and I did this in a two-step process. Moved the existing card stacks.
Then, added and implemented the Undo button.
